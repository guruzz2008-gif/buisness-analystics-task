# 📊 Employee Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
The Employee Analytics Dashboard provides a visual analysis of employee data across different departments, cities, experience levels, performance scores, annual salaries, and satisfaction scores.

The dashboard helps understand workforce distribution, department-wise employee trends, employee performance, and city-wise departmental patterns through interactive visualizations.

## 📈 Dashboard Sheets

### Sheet 1 – Department-wise Employee Distribution
- IT and Sales departments have the highest number of employees.
- Operations has a significant employee count.
- Finance, HR, and Marketing have comparatively fewer employees.
- IT and Sales represent major portions of the workforce.

### Sheet 2 – Monthly Employee Trends
- Displays monthly employee counts across different departments.
- IT shows noticeable fluctuations throughout the year.
- Sales maintains relatively high employee counts during several months.
- HR and Finance have comparatively lower monthly counts.
- Helps identify department-wise workforce trends over time.

### Sheet 3 – Employee Performance Analysis
- Analyzes the relationship between employee experience and performance scores.
- Most employees have performance scores between 60 and 90.
- Employees with different experience levels show varying performance scores.
- Annual salary is represented using bubble sizes.
- Department-wise comparison helps understand employee performance patterns.

### Sheet 4 – City-wise Department Analysis
- Compares department-level data across different cities.
- Includes Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Kolkata, Mumbai, and Pune.
- Highlights differences in departmental values across locations.
- Helps identify city-wise workforce patterns.

## 🔍 Key Findings – What Did I Discover?

1. IT and Sales are the largest departments in terms of employee count.
2. Operations also contributes a significant number of employees.
3. Employee performance scores are mostly concentrated between 60 and 90.
4. Employee experience and performance show variations across individuals.
5. Monthly employee counts differ across departments.
6. Departmental values vary across the eight cities analyzed.

## 📝 Conclusion

The Employee Analytics Dashboard provides useful insights into workforce distribution, employee performance, monthly department trends, and city-wise departmental patterns.

The analysis indicates that IT and Sales have the largest employee populations, while employee performance varies across different experience levels.

Overall, this dashboard helps organizations understand their workforce and make better data-driven HR decisions.
